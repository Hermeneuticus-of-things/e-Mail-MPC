# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- being respectful and constructive
- accepting feedback graciously
- focusing on technical and product quality
- helping others learn and improve

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment, intimidation, or abusive behavior
- discriminatory language or conduct
- personal attacks or hostile debate
- deliberate disruption of technical collaboration

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards. Violations may result in removal from participation in the project at the maintainers' discretion.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces and in public spaces when representing the project.

## Reporting

Please report violations to the project maintainer through the official repository contact or security route.
